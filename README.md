## Project Background｜專題背景

Traffic accidents are an important public safety issue. Through data analysis and visualization, users can better understand differences in accident casualties across regions, vehicle types, age groups, gender, and accident-related factors.

交通事故是重要的公共安全議題。透過資料分析與視覺化，可以協助使用者快速理解不同縣市、車種、年齡層、性別與事故因素之間的差異。

This project was created as a graduation project to demonstrate the use of data processing, chart generation, and web-based presentation for public issue analysis.

本專案作為大學畢業專題成果，主要展示如何運用資料整理、圖表產生與網頁呈現方式，分析公共議題並保存專題成果。

## Visualization Scope｜視覺化分析範圍

本專案從縣市、年份、車種、性別、年齡層、肇逃案件與事故因素等不同面向呈現交通事故統計資料。

The visualization results include:

視覺化內容包含：

  各縣市交通事故統計

- Fatality statistics from 2018 to 2022  
  2018 至 2022 年死亡人數統計

- Vehicle-type fatality comparison  
  各車種死亡人數比較

- Gender-based fatality comparison  
  男女死亡比例分析

- Hit-and-run statistics by city and county  
  各縣市肇逃人數分析

- Age-group accident distribution  
  事故發生年齡層分布

  事故因素與類型視覺化

## Project Findings｜專題觀察

Based on the original project analysis, several observations were highlighted:

根據原專題分析結果，系統展示中包含以下觀察：

- Motorcycle-related fatalities were relatively high in the 2018–2022 data.  
  在 2018 至 2022 年資料中，機車相關事故死亡人數相對較高。

- Pedestrian fatalities were identified as an important traffic safety issue to observe.  
  行人事故死亡情形亦是值得重視的交通安全議題。

- Accident statistics showed differences across city or county, vehicle type, gender, and age group.  
  事故統計在縣市、車種、性別與年齡層等面向呈現差異。

- The visualization website was designed to help users understand traffic safety risks more intuitively.  
  視覺化網站設計目標在於協助使用者更直覺地理解交通安全風險。
